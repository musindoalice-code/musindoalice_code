# 🔍 How I Think About Data

When I receive a new dataset, I don't immediately start building visuals.

I ask:

### 01 — What is the business problem?

What decision are we trying to support?

### 02 — What does the data represent?

What is the grain, population and time period?

### 03 — Can I trust the data?

I check:

* Missing values
* Duplicates
* Invalid values
* Data types
* Outliers
* Relationships
* Consistency

### 04 — What should we measure?

I define KPIs that connect directly to the business question.

### 05 — What changed?

I investigate:

* Trends
* Variances
* Segments
* Products
* Stores
* Customers
* Time periods

### 06 — Why did it change?

I investigate potential drivers rather than stopping at the headline number.

### 07 — What should the stakeholder know?

I translate technical analysis into clear business language.
